# vc4_y_bumper_depressor

Depressor made from modified VC4 Y bumper. use a small washer with the M4 to keep the lever on the body.
on IDEX machines there's plenty of 'room for activities' on either side of the print surface.
the depressor on this is magnetic, this way we can home and probe as usual and the lever simply pushes out of the way.
When a cut opperation needs to happen, simply make a gcode macro that goes around and pull the crossbow lever.
disclaimer, be sure the order of homing, etc never comes at the lever from the depressing side.
Usually this isn't a probem because of the extra space on IDEX. extra caution on non idex machines.

BOM:
2x 10x4 magnets (common ratrig enclosure panel mounting, etc.)
1x M4x6mm ~ fastener
